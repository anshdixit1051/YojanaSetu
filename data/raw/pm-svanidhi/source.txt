PM STREET VENDOR'S ATMANIRBHAR NIDHI (PM SVANIDHI)

SCHEME GUIDELINES

Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs
Government of India

---

Section 1 – Background and Purpose
The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs has launched PM SVANidhi, a special micro-credit facility scheme for providing affordable working capital loan to street vendors to resume their livelihoods that have been adversely affected due to Covid-19 lockdowns.

---

PM SVANidhi Scheme Guidelines – Eligibility

Section 3 – Eligibility of Beneficiaries:
The scheme is available to all street vendors engaged in vending in urban areas as on or before March 24, 2020. The eligible vendors are identified as per the following criteria:
(i) Street Vendor Status: The applicant must be a street vendor vending in urban or surrounding peri-urban/rural areas on or before 24th March 2020.
(ii) Vending Certificate or Identity: Street vendors in possession of Certificate of Vending / Identity Card issued by Urban Local Bodies (ULBs), or vendors who have been identified in the survey but have not been issued Certificate of Vending / Identity Card, or vendors who possess a Letter of Recommendation (LoR) issued by the ULB / Town Vending Committee (TVC). The vendor must have a valid vending certificate, ID, or LoR.
(iii) Active Loan Status: The applicant must not have an existing active default or duplicate active PM SVANidhi first-tranche loan pending disbursement or settlement. Beneficiaries must not already possess an active unsettled first tranche SVANidhi loan facility.

---

Section 4 – Salient Features of the Scheme
- Initial working capital loan of up to Rs. 10,000 for a tenure of 1 year.
- On timely or early repayment, the vendor is eligible for enhanced credit limit in subsequent tranches (Rs. 20,000 in second tranche and Rs. 50,000 in third tranche).
- Rate of interest subsidy at 7% per annum credited directly to beneficiary's bank account.
