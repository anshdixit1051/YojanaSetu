POST-MATRIC SCHOLARSHIP FOR SC STUDENTS

SCHEME GUIDELINES

Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment
Government of India

---

Clause 1 – Objective
The objective of the scheme is to appreciably increase the Gross Enrolment Ratio of SC students in higher education, with a focus on those from the poorest households, by providing financial assistance at post-matriculation or post-secondary stage.

---

NSP Post-Matric SC Scholarship Guidelines – Eligibility

Clause 4 – Eligibility Criteria:
(a) Caste Category: The applicant must belong to the Scheduled Caste (SC) category as specified in the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order applicable to the concerned State/UT.
(b) Income Limit: Scholarships will be paid to students whose parents/guardians' all-source annual family income does not exceed Rs. 2,50,000/- (Rupees Two Lakh Fifty Thousand only) per annum.
(c) Post-Matric Level: The applicant must be studying at the post-matriculation or post-secondary level (Class 11 and above, including technical, vocational, degree, postgraduate, and professional courses) in a recognized institution.
(d) Domicile: The candidate must be a permanent resident and domicile of the respective State/UT from which the application is submitted. In this deployment, the applicant must be a domicile of Uttar Pradesh.

---

Clause 5 – Conditions of Award
(i) Candidates who have passed one stage of education and are studying in the same stage of education in a different subject will not be eligible.
(ii) All children of the same parents/guardian are eligible for the scholarship.
(iii) Scholarship will not be given to more than two pupils belonging to the same parents for technical degree courses, except in case of girl students.
