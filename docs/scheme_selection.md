portal (e.g. myScheme.gov.in) before collecting text. Rules come only from text
pasted into `data/raw/<scheme-id>/source.txt`.

Source priority: guidelines PDF / gazette > ministry portal page > nothing else
(no blogs or news sites). Paste verbatim, UTF-8, no edits.

Selection rules:
- At least 3 schemes with vague or discretionary criteria (to demo "Needs More Info").
- Prefer schemes with an official Hindi version.
- 3 to 4 slots are reserved for state schemes once the state is chosen.
