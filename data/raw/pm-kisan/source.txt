PRADHAN MANTRI KISAN SAMMAN NIDHI (PM-KISAN)

OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES (REVISED)

Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
Government of India

---

1. BACKGROUND

The Central Government has launched a new Scheme, Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAmman NIidhi (PM-KISAN), vide Gazette Notification dated 24.02.2019. Under this scheme, an income support of Rs.6000/- per year will be provided to all landholding farmer families across the country, in three equal instalments of Rs.2000/- each. The fund will be directly transferred to the bank accounts of the beneficiary farmers.

---

2. ELIGIBILITY

All landholding farmer families, which have cultivable land, are eligible under the scheme, subject to the following exclusion criteria.

---

3. DEFINITION OF FARMER FAMILY

Under the scheme, 'Farmer Family' means a family comprising of husband, wife and minor children who own cultivable land as per land records of the concerned State/UT. The existing land-ownership system in a State/UT will be used for identification of beneficiaries under the scheme.

---

4. FINANCIAL BENEFIT

An amount of Rs 6,000/- per year per family will be paid in three instalments of Rs 2,000/- each, every four months.

---

5. CATEGORIES NOT ELIGIBLE UNDER PM-KISAN SCHEME

The following categories of beneficiaries of higher economic status shall not be eligible for benefit under the scheme:

(i) All Institutional Land holders.

(ii) Farmer families in which one or more of its members belong to following categories:
  a. Former and present holders of constitutional posts
  b. Former and present Ministers/ State Ministers and former/present Members of LokSabha/ RajyaSabha/ State Legislative Assemblies/ State Legislative Councils, former and present Mayors of Municipal Corporations, former and present Chairpersons of District Panchayats.
  c. All serving or retired officers and employees of Central/ State Government Ministries /Offices/Departments and its field units Central or State PSEs and Attached offices /Autonomous Institutions under Government as well as regular employees of the Local Bodies (Excluding Multi Tasking Staff /Class IV/Group D employees)
  d. All superannuated/retired pensioners whose monthly pension is Rs.10,000/- or more (Excluding Multi Tasking Staff / Class IV/Group D employees) of above category
  e. All Persons who paid Income Tax in last assessment year
  f. Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, and Architects registered with Professional bodies and carrying out profession by undertaking practices.

---

6. SCHEME COVERAGE

The scheme was initially for small and marginal farmers with cultivable land holding upto 2 hectares. This was later extended to cover all farmer families irrespective of the size of their land holdings with effect from 01.06.2019.

---

7. IMPLEMENTATION MODALITY

(i) Identification of eligible farmer families, uploading of data pertaining to such farmers, is primarily the responsibility of the State/UT Government.
(ii) The States/UTs will be required to identify the farmer families, who are eligible as per the scheme guidelines.
(iii) The States/UTs Governments shall ensure that benefits are not extended to ineligible beneficiaries.
(iv) The State/UT Governments will be responsible for correctness of data uploaded.
(v) The State/UT Government will upload data of eligible farmers on PM-KISAN portal.
(vi) After funds are released by GoI, the same will be transferred directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries through PFMS.

---

8. REGISTRATION

Farmers can register themselves on the PM-KISAN portal (www.pmkisan.gov.in) or through Common Service Centres (CSCs).
