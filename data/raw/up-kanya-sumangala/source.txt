MUKHYA MANTRI KANYA SUMANGALA YOJANA

GOVERNMENT OF UTTAR PRADESH
Department of Women and Child Development

---

Clause 1 – Introduction
The Government of Uttar Pradesh has introduced the Mukhya Mantri Kanya Sumangala Yojana to establish gender equality, eliminate female feticide, strengthen girl education, and support financial security for girls from birth until completion of higher education.

---

UP Kanya Sumangala Yojana Guidelines – Eligibility

Clause 3 – Eligibility Criteria for Beneficiaries:
The benefits under the scheme shall be payable subject to the fulfillment of the following eligibility conditions:
(i) Beneficiary Gender: The beneficiary must be a girl child (female).
(ii) Domicile: The beneficiary's family must be permanent residents and domiciles of Uttar Pradesh, having residence proof such as Aadhaar, Voter Card, Ration Card, or Domicile Certificate issued by the competent authority in Uttar Pradesh.
(iii) Income Ceiling: The annual family income of the beneficiary's family from all sources must not exceed Rs. 3,00,000/- (Rupees Three Lakh only) per annum.
(iv) Maximum Children: The benefit of the scheme shall be admissible to a maximum of two girl children in a single family. In case of twins in the second delivery, the third girl child shall also be eligible as an exception.

---

Clause 4 – Stages of Assistance
Assistance is disbursed in six categories:
Stage 1: Rs. 2,000 on birth.
Stage 2: Rs. 1,000 on full immunization within 1 year.
Stage 3: Rs. 2,000 on admission to Class 1.
Stage 4: Rs. 2,000 on admission to Class 6.
Stage 5: Rs. 3,000 on admission to Class 9.
Stage 6: Rs. 5,000 on passing Class 10/12 and admission into degree or at least 2-year diploma course.
